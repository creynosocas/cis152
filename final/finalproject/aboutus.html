About the trails

Nestled along the serene coastline, Pacific Trails Resort offers a unique blend of luxury and nature. Our trails wind through ancient forests leading right to the ocean's edge, you will be charmed with a beautiful view no matter the season you decide to visit us.

Our Story

Founded by the Walker family in 1998, the resort began as a small eco-lodge. Their vision was to create a space where comfort meets conservation. Ecological practices and conserving the space around us is fundamental, to be able to enjoy what nature has given us is to care about it and be respectful. Passed through generations, now is in charge by Allan and Mia Walker. We are more than happy to welcome you since our costumers are priority; where diversity and comfort is part of the environment. Creating a safe space for you and your loved ones, today and tomorrow.
